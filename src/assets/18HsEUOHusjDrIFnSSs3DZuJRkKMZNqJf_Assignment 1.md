Symbol of yin and yang is created by Chinese man called Zhao Huiqian (1351-1395 CE). Many of philosophers think about the purpose or the meaning behind this symbol. Some of them see that it describes our nature and other see that it describe the purpose of our life and this what makes it as art as everybody see it and understand its meaning by his own mind. I see that it’s great art as it’s simple circle with only two colors: black and white although I see that it has a fantastic meaning as white and black represent goodness and evil and half black circle has a small white circle and in the other hand half white circle has a small one with black color, too. Thus, this is art because it has an unknown message which push you to think about it. In my opinion that message says that evil people have their light side same as good people has their dark side and from this the balance is approached. This is the art of yin and yang.
